PlanAIBoard 0.2.0 (macOS only)

A local dashboard for people who use AI assistants on their Mac: it finds the AI programs installed, shows everything it found, links them to your folders and lets you choose what to use. It runs only on your computer.

1. Double-click avvia.command. Python 3 is required: if your Mac does not have it, install it from python.org. The first time, macOS may block the file: right-click avvia.command -> Open -> Open.
2. The page opens: follow the tour.

Privacy: the app sends nothing outside your computer. Only if you use the optional assistant, a summary of names (never file contents) goes to the AI you choose, after your consent.
Your choices are stored in impostazioni.json (you can edit it by hand). Hooking into AI programs always makes a backup copy (.bak) and can be undone.
